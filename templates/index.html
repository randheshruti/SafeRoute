About us

Our mission

The fastest route is not always the safest one. SafeRoute was created to help everyday travellers — students, commuters and people travelling alone — choose routes with more awareness and less worry.

By combining route planning with community incident reports, safe-place information and emergency contacts, SafeRoute puts useful safety information in one simple app.

Questions & answers

How does SafeRoute choose the safest route?

It gets alternative routes, then counts reported incidents and risk zones within 300 m of each route. The route with the fewest hazards is marked as safest.

Does the SOS button call the police?

No. It saves a record with your location and shows buttons to call your own emergency contacts. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

How do I add safe places or risk zones?

Admins can add them from the Admin page by clicking on the map.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are stored as hashes and only logged-in users can see map data and submit reports.
